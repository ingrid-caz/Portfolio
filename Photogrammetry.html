Photogrammetry, 2026

During a three day workshop, we discovered photogrammetry, a technique that involves taking a large number of photographs of an object in order to generate a usable 3D model of it. I chose to work with plants because they are difficult to model in 3D and can have interesting translucent properties. To emphasise those properties, I used a point cloud representation for my 3D model and made some changes to the colour and movement.

Softwares : Blender, Reality Scan, Touchdesigner
